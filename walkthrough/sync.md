# Sync a folder

1. Right-click a bucket or folder and choose "Sync Folder".
2. Pick the direction (local folder to S3, or S3 to a local folder) and the folder.
3. Choose "Keep extra files" or "Mirror" (Mirror also deletes what is not in the source).
4. A dry-run plan lists what is new, changed and to be deleted. Nothing changes until you press "Apply". Deletes and replacements are listed once more before they happen.

"Plan a Recent Sync Again" in the view's menu repeats a sync you ran before.
